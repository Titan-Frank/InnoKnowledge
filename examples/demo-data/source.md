# Synthetic Home Solar Learning Module

This repository-authored module exists only to demonstrate traceable knowledge objects and is not adapted from a textbook.

## 1. Energy path

### 1.1 Sunlight resource
Sunlight carries radiant energy that can reach a solar panel without being produced by the panel itself.

### 1.2 Solar panel
A solar panel receives sunlight and provides electrical energy at its output when operating conditions allow conversion.

### 1.3 Photovoltaic conversion
Photovoltaic conversion changes part of the incoming radiant energy into electrical energy inside a solar panel.

### 1.4 Electrical energy
Electrical energy is the transferable energy carried by electric charge through a circuit.

### 1.5 Energy-flow diagram
An energy-flow diagram represents sunlight entering a panel, conversion inside it, and electrical energy leaving it.

## 2. Conditions and estimation

### 2.1 Rated power
Rated power states the electrical output power assigned to a device under specified reference conditions.

### 2.2 Low-sunlight period
A low-sunlight period reduces the radiant input available for photovoltaic conversion and can lower electrical output.

### 2.3 Daily energy estimation
Daily energy estimation multiplies an appropriate power estimate by operating time while accounting for changing conditions.

### 2.4 Energy-balance rule
An energy-balance rule requires every estimate to distinguish input energy, useful output energy, and conversion losses.

Every sentence above was written for this software demo; the values and relationships are illustrative rather than engineering design advice.
